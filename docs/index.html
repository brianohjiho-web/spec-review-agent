Spec review for power equipment

A customer sends a spec that lists what they need. SpecCheck reads it and checks each item against your products, so you see fast which product fits and what is missing.

  1. Add the customer's spec PDF.
  2. Claude finds each requirement and copies the exact words from the spec.
  3. Simple rules compare each one to your catalog: pass, fail, or review.
  4. You get the best product, the gaps, and questions for the customer.
